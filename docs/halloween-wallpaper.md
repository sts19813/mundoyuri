# Portada Halloween responsive

Original preservado: `public/assets/img/wallpaper halloween.webp` (432 × 432).
Ediciones creadas con la herramienta integrada de imágenes, usando el original como objetivo.

- Escritorio: `public/assets/img/seasonal/halloween-hero-desktop.webp`, 1672 × 941, 85 KB.
- Móvil: `public/assets/img/seasonal/halloween-hero-mobile.webp`, 768 × 1152, 54 KB.

Se conservan los dos personajes, la pose y el estilo de ilustración. Se reconstruyó el
trazo para mejorar la nitidez y se extendió el fondo en ciruela oscuro para permitir
texto legible. La variante móvil tiene una composición propia, no un recorte del escritorio.
Las imágenes se seleccionan mediante `picture` a 768 px y se precarga solo la correspondiente.
Con Halloween activo se omite el vídeo de portada; desactivarlo restaura las imágenes
y el vídeo configurados en Inicio y portada. La integración sigue limitada al entorno local.

## Prompt de escritorio

Use case: compositing. Input image 1 is the edit target. Improve and recompose this exact anime Halloween illustration as a refined website hero background. Preserve the same two female characters, their recognizable faces, hairstyles, witch hat with bow, cat ears, purple costumes, pink spiral lollipop, and affectionate pose. Preserve the original delicate muted anime ink-and-cel illustration style; restore crisp clean linework and smooth color shapes from the small compressed original, without photo-realism or glossy 3D. Extend the canvas with restrained midnight plum atmosphere blending into the original muted peach Halloween palette near the characters. No additional characters, no spiders, no cobwebs, no bats. No added logos, captions, typography, greeting text or signatures. Finished opaque background image, not a mockup. Output composition: wide landscape 16:9, approximately 1920x1080 or larger. Keep BOTH characters and their faces comfortably within the RIGHT 44 percent of the frame, complete witch hat and ears inside frame with safe margin. Extend their lower costumes naturally towards the lower right as needed without changing the pose. Leave LEFT 55 percent quiet and very dark plum (#100b16) with extremely subtle painted gradients, low detail and no bright objects so white title text can be placed there. Bottom edge fades naturally to dark plum. Do not render the title. Keep the important character details in the middle 75 percent of image height, safe for cover cropping.
